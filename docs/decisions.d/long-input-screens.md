## 2026-11-22 · The longest input screens keep their sections in view

The plain-wording pass (queue item 26) measured the input screens with
explanations on: the longest (two groups, paired, ANCOVA, one-way ANOVA,
multiple regression) show about 550 to 650 words, about 40 of them
navigation. Their help already meets the wording rule, so shortening them
further would mean folding sections that don't apply to the chosen method
(for example the simulation settings on a card that calculates by
formula) behind a heading. Decided: leave the screens as they are, with
every section in view; explanations off remains the way to see a shorter
screen.
