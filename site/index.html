Powerkit

Power analysis and sample size planning for research studies.

Describe your study once, in plain terms, and Powerkit works out the power, the sample size you need, or the smallest effect you could detect. It routes each design to established R packages and documented methods, and explains each input as you go.

Development prototype. Validation is ongoing. Check the evidence and limitations for your exact analysis before using an answer to plan a real study.

Open Powerkit

It opens in your web browser, with nothing to install. After a quiet spell the app needs up to a minute to start.

What it covers

Common t-tests, correlations, ANOVA and ANCOVA, regression, proportions and categorical outcomes, rank tests, moderation and mediation, clustered and multilevel designs, selected structural equation models, reliability, meta-analysis and precision questions. Coverage varies by outcome, test version and design. Some calculations run in the app; for others it gives a preview and an R script to run yourself.

If you don't know the name of your analysis, “Help me choose” asks about your study and finds it. “Plan my whole study” handles several analyses at once.

Why use it

  • Results come from established R packages, including pwr, pwrss, Superpower, Spower, power4mome, InteractionPoweR, powertools, TOSTER, semPower and bfpwr. Where Powerkit implements a formula or simulation itself, the method, source and checks are documented.
  • It checks that the population you describe is possible before calculating.
  • Every result records how it was produced (backend, method, seed and software versions) and comes with an R script that reproduces it with the backend package alone.
  • Each result gives a methods paragraph and the citations for the package and its method paper.
  • Projects and study plans save as files you keep. The app keeps nothing on its server between visits.
