* `replication_planning(allocation_ratio =)`: a replication of two groups can have unequal groups (group 2's size over group 1's, as the cards' `options > allocation_ratio`). Each total is split into whole groups by the cards' rule (`rounded_groups()`), every goal is judged at those groups' standard error sqrt(1/n1 + 1/n2), and the sample size is the smallest total whose groups reach it. The Bayes factor t-tests use bfpwr's `ptbf01()` at the two groups (its `ntbf01()` searches equal groups only), the smallest total found by bisection; small telescopes runs the card with the same ratio. `goals` gains `n1` and `n2`. BUCSS's correction plans equal groups only, so `bias_corrected` is `NULL` for unequal ones. `replication_planning_script()` reproduces the numbers, and `replication_recruitment(allocation_ratio =)` allows for dropout in each group (queue item 28).
