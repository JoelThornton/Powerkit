* `replication_planning(reliability_original =, reliability_replication =)` now takes correlation originals too. An observed r is the true r times the square root of both measures' reliabilities (Schmidt & Hunter, 1996), so the reliabilities given are those of the measure that changes (or the products of both measures' reliabilities when both change); r is scaled by the square root of their ratio, moved back to Fisher's z, and its standard error scaled so that its z is unchanged, as for d. A simulation test checks the attenuation of r. `replication_planning_script()` does the same. Pairs (d_z), where measurement error also changes the correlation between the two measurements, are still not covered (queue item 28).
