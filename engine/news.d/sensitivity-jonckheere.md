* The smallest detectable effect (goal `sensitivity`) for the
  Jonckheere-Terpstra trend test, by the simulated search
  (`simulated_effect_sensitivity()`): the pattern of group means is scaled
  about its average, the SD and the score shape kept, and the result is
  reported as the difference between the largest and smallest mean. A flat
  pattern is refused plainly. The exported script repeats the search; the
  test checks the answer against a fresh simulation with 4,000 replications
  and another seed, and runs the script.
