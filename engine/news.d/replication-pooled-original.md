* `replication_planning(original_analysis = "pooled")`: the original is the pooled estimate of several studies or a meta-analysis, given as d (d_z for pairs) or r with its standard error (`original_se`) or confidence interval (`original_ci`, `original_ci_level`), and no sample size. Every goal is judged against that estimate and standard error, as for a Bayesian original; small telescopes and BUCSS need one study's test and sample size and are left out. For r, a standard error of r is moved to Fisher's z with the delta method (se / (1 - r^2)) and an interval is transformed end by end. `replication_planning_script()` writes the same (queue item 28).
