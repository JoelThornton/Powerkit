* `replication_planning(reliability_original =, reliability_replication =)`: for two groups or one group, a replication whose outcome measure is more or less reliable than the original's. An observed d is the true d times the square root of the measure's reliability (Schmidt & Hunter, 1996, added to refs.bib), so the original's estimate and standard error are multiplied by sqrt(reliability_replication / reliability_original) before every goal (its z is unchanged); a simulation test checks the attenuation. Small telescopes and BUCSS work in the original's own units and are left out when the reliabilities differ. Pairs (d_z, where error also changes the correlation between the measurements) and correlations (both measures' reliabilities, on Fisher's z) are not covered yet. `replication_planning_script()` scales the same way (queue item 28).
